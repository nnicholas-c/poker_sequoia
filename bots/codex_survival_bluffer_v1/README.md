# Survival + Empty Bluffer v1

Adds low-card exposure and bounded small prize bets to the survival/paid-place policy. Keeps made hands, correctness fixes and the switch to stronger pressure after cashing. Extra prize bets are limited to 6% of remaining stack before cashing and 10% afterward, never all-in.

See [actual prize counts and tournament tradeoffs](../../arena/benchmarks/prize-fusion/README.md). This is a local tested candidate, not a guarantee of any prize or a live upload.
